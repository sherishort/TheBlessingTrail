Julie’s Place — Feels Like Home

For Julie at Julie’s Place, Murphy, NC · Stop #1 · Written October 4, 2026

Julie’s Place, come on in,
There’s always room for one more friend.
Breakfast, lunch and dinner, made with love each day,
Good old-fashioned cooking, served the Southern way.

Come in as a stranger, leave as family,
Pull up a chair, you’re right where you should be.
Good food, warm hearts, blessings from above,
At Julie’s Place, you’re always served with love.

Julie’s Place…
Where it feels like home.
