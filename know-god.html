Know God

The road that matters most

Every stop on this trail points back to one thing: God's love, and the road He opened for us to know Him. If you've never taken that step, here's the path — sometimes called the Romans Road, because every step is a verse from the book of Romans.

Romans 3:23

Everyone has sinned and fallen short of who God made us to be.

Romans 6:23

Sin leads to death, but God offers eternal life as a free gift through Jesus Christ.

Romans 5:8

God proved His love for us — Christ died for us while we were still sinners.

Romans 10:9

If you declare with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved.

Romans 10:13

Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.

A Prayer to Begin

Dear God, I know I've sinned and fallen short of You. I believe Jesus died for my sins and rose again. I turn away from my sin and ask Jesus to be Lord of my life. Thank You for saving me and walking this road with me from here on out. Amen.
