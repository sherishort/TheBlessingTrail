Our Mission

We're hitting the road to see where God leads

We're setting out with no fixed destination — just a willingness to stop wherever God tells us to stop, and get to know whoever we meet along the way. Wherever we're blessed to be able to, we'll bless someone else: a cup of coffee, a hand carrying in groceries, a little while just sitting and listening. Small things, done on purpose.

We're keeping this site updated as we go — not to boast, but to show how easy it really is to bless somebody. Every blessing logged here is anonymous. It's not about us or the person we met — it's about the count of good things going out into the world, one stop at a time.
